About Me

First graduate from Grand Valley State University with a B.A.S. in Web Design and Development, combining a background in Graphic Communications with user-centered development.

I am a UX/UI Designer driven by the challenge of turning complex problems into clean, intuitive digital experiences. My approach blends a background in graphic design with a focus on user-centered development, ensuring that every project I touch is as functional as it is visually engaging.

My journey in design began in Southeast Michigan, where I discovered my love for coding at Oakland Schools Technical Campus, which blossomed into my Associate’s at Oakland Community College. From there, I went west to pursue my Bachelor’s at Grand Valley State University. Being part of the GVSU community, especially its Swim Club, has allowed me to sharpen my technical skills while collaborating with like-minded people on real-world solutions.

When I’m not pushing pixels or refining code, you can usually find me swimming, hiking, or enjoying a good cup of coffee. I believe that a great designer never stops wondering—whether inside a web builder or out in the world.
