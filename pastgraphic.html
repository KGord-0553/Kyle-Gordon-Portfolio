Graphic & 3D Design

Alongside web and app development, I create visual media ranging from branding and expressive typography to real-time 3D models. Explore selected graphic design projects created in Adobe Creative Suite and WebGL below.

Steve The Snowman

Medium: 3D Modeling & WebGL ┃ Computer Graphics Capstone

Steve the Snowman is an interactive 3D model developed as the capstone project for my Computer Graphics course. Beyond geometry creation, this project required technical implementation of dynamic lighting, realistic shadow mapping, and a user-controlled orbital camera system.

To add personality and test interactivity, I programmed custom animations allowing Steve to tip his hat, bridging creative character design with complex graphics programming.

Expressive Typography

Medium: Typography & Layout ┃ Graphic Design

Created during my Graphic Design coursework, this piece deconstructs a quote from a video game into a standalone visual narrative. The goal was conveying narrative emotion using typography, hierarchy, and color alone.

By manipulating font weight, tracking, and a custom dawn-inspired color palette, the typography evokes the feeling of a morning horizon, proving how text styling reinforces messaging beyond basic readability.

Maui Poster Design

Medium: Print & Digital Media ┃ Adobe Illustrator / Photoshop

Designed as a focal print piece, this poster serves as a travel tribute to the island of Maui. The creative process involved combining high-resolution environment imagery with curated typography reflecting the vibrant energy of the tropics.

I utilized layer masking and depth-of-field techniques to weave text behind foreground elements, while embedding subtle micro-details (including a hidden fun fact in the sand texture) for attentive viewers.
