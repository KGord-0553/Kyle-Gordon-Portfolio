Past Projects

I've been coding for a while, so over time my experience has added up to a lot of different computer creations. Below here I have links to the websites I've had a hand in making or created on my own, and even a small text-based adventure game I created in Python!

Occurency

An event planning website built for my senior Web Application Programming project, where I served as the Lead UI Designer.

For my senior Web Application Programming project, I served as the Lead UI Designer for Occurency. Beyond just making the site look good, I was responsible for bridging the gap between complex back-end functionality and a user-friendly front-end. I developed the CSS framework that maintains brand consistency across core pages and focuses on intuitive navigation to ensure users could plan events with zero friction. This project sharpened my ability to translate conceptual requirements into a polished, functional digital product.

Visit Occurency HERE!

Crocs Swim Log

An Android app designed to help swimmers of all skill levels log workouts, where I designed the UI.

Crocs Swim Log was born out of a desire to simplify the way athletes track their performance. As a national level swimmer myself and as an aspiring designer, I felt inspired by this project like no other. I was challenged to rethink the user journey for someone who is likely at the pool and needs to input data with zero friction. I prioritized a clean, bottom-navigation flow and a bold, energetic color palette to match the intensity of the sport I love. This project allowed me to explore the structural differences between web and mobile design, ultimately teaching me how to craft an experience that feels native, intuitive, and responsive to the user's immediate needs.

Visit Crocs Swim Log HERE!

explAIn

An AI Chatbot designed for ease of use for a 50+ year old audience. I served as Lead Designer for the Project.

explAIn was born out of a fascinating final group project for my Writing for the Web class, which focused on the way websites can bring out stories and audiences. The goal was to create the frontend for an AI website, like ChatGPT or Google Gemini, and make it cater more towards an 50+ year old audience. Taking this idea, I oversaw the overall design of the website, keeping the color scheme simple but striking and the flow clean and clear with a standard top to bottom approach. It pushed me as a designer to truly understand the target demographic of what I'm desgining, and come up with visually appealing and well functioning solutions.
